Create three files in /app:

1. `report.md` — a Markdown report that contains the exact line
   `RewardKit render demo` and at least 60 words of text overall.
2. `summary.txt` — exactly three lines of text.
3. `data/metrics.csv` — a CSV with the header `metric,value` and at least
   two data rows, one of which must be for the metric `cpu_seconds`.

Keep the content simple; the verifier only checks the structure above.
