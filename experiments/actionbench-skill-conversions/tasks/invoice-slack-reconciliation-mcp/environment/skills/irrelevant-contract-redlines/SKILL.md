# Contract Redline Review

This skill applies to legal document review, not finance ledger reconciliation.

Use it when the task requires:

- comparing two versions of a contract
- identifying clause edits
- summarizing negotiation risk
- preparing a legal redline memo

It is not useful for parsing spreadsheets, reconciling Slack correction tokens, computing invoice balances, or producing finance summaries.
